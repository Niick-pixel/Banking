# Aufwind: 10 upgrades (v0.8 onward)

The goal: an app that explains itself, shows less but clearer, and keeps your numbers right with little effort.
Each phase was started in v0.8. "Started" means its first useful slice ships now; "Next" is what follows.

| # | Phase | Shipped in v0.8 | Next |
|---|-------|-----------------|------|
| 1 | **Declutter the layout** | Menu shows labels only (explanations moved to hover tooltips), narrower sidebar, sync status as a dot | Collapsible sidebar (icons only); shorter page subtitles everywhere |
| 2 | **Budget you can read at a glance** | Budget overview: 4 key numbers (spent, budget, on-track ring, income), monthly comparison chart, one clean category table (months, % of budget, trend, open movements); the workbook year table kept as a toggle | Click a category for its own page (history, average, merchants); budget templates per quincena |
| 3 | **Credit cards are debt** | All cards together as one widget (total owed, per card, due dates, limit used), on Debts and the dashboard; counted in "Total owed"; Pay and Edit (limit, rate, due/statement day) | Card statement cycle view (what's in this statement vs next); interest warning before the due date |
| 4 | **Everything explained** | A "?" next to key numbers (dashboard KPIs, Budget, Debts, Cards) with plain-language definitions | "?" on every card and chart; a glossary page (quincena, free cash, avalanche, utilization…) |
| 5 | **Guided setup** | "Get set up" checklist first on the dashboard until done: accounts, cards, budgets, bills, check-in, data folder | Short guided tours per page the first time you open it |
| 6 | **Polish & bug sweep** | Fixed: stretched bar in the Debts card, "Over" shown at exactly 100% (now "At budget"), the bell badge contrast | Consistent empty states and loading placeholders; number formats (₡185k) in tight spaces |
| 7 | **Speed** | Every page except Home loads on first open (smaller start) | Precomputed monthly totals shared by all pages; faster large-history scrolling |
| 8 | **Faster editing** | Transactions: select a whole month, see the selection's total, change category, account or date, or delete in bulk (with undo) | Rules: "always put Uber in Transportation"; split a movement across categories from the list |
| 9 | **Pay off debt smarter** | Avalanche/snowball now include your credit cards (rate from Edit, minimum ≈ 5% or ₡10,000) | A month-by-month payoff calendar; "what if I pay ₡X more" on each card |
| 10 | **A quincena routine** | Quincena review page (Home → Quincena review): balances, bills to confirm, data fixes, over-budget, Coach warnings, next 15 days; reminder in the bell | Monthly email-style summary (PDF) and a year-end review |
